# Ivy 1988 - Muscle Glycogen Synthesis After Exercise

## Source

- Title: Muscle glycogen synthesis after exercise: effect of time of carbohydrate ingestion
- Authors: J L Ivy, A L Katz, C L Cutler, W M Sherman, E F Coyle
- Journal: Journal of Applied Physiology, 1988 Apr;64(4):1480-1485
- PMID: 3132449
- DOI: 10.1152/jappl.1988.64.4.1480
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3132449/

## Summary

這篇研究檢驗運動後碳水補充的時間點是否影響肌糖原儲存速率。12 名男性自行車手在兩次實驗中完成相同的耗竭性自行車運動，之後分別在運動後立即或延後 2 小時攝取 25% 碳水溶液，劑量為 2 g/kg body weight。研究者在運動後 0、2、4 小時採取股外側肌肉切片，並搭配血液樣本觀察血糖與胰島素反應。

主要結果是：運動後立即攝取碳水，在前 2 小時的肌糖原儲存速率明顯高於延後 2 小時攝取。延後組在第 3-4 小時的儲存速率上升，但仍低於立即組前 2 小時的速率。作者結論是，延後運動後碳水補充會降低早期肌糖原儲存速率。

## Study Design

- Participants: 12 male cyclists.
- Exercise protocol: 70 min continuous cycling at 68% VO2max, interrupted by six 2-min intervals at 88% VO2max.
- Conditions:
  - Immediate carbohydrate post-exercise.
  - Carbohydrate delayed until 2 h post-exercise.
- Intervention: 25% carbohydrate solution, 2 g/kg body weight.
- Measurements:
  - Vastus lateralis muscle biopsies at 0, 2, and 4 h post-exercise.
  - Blood samples before, during, and after exercise.

## Key Findings

- Post-exercise muscle glycogen levels were not significantly different between conditions at baseline recovery.
- During the first 2 h post-exercise, immediate carbohydrate produced a glycogen storage rate of 7.7 umol/g wet weight/h.
- During the same first 2 h, delayed carbohydrate produced a glycogen storage rate of 2.5 umol/g wet weight/h.
- During hours 2-4, immediate carbohydrate slowed to 4.3 umol/g wet weight/h.
- During hours 2-4, delayed carbohydrate increased to 4.1 umol/g wet weight/h.
- The delayed condition remained slower than the early post-exercise window in the immediate condition, despite elevated plasma glucose and insulin.

## Practical Interpretation

如果運動員需要在短時間內恢復肌糖原，例如一天兩練、賽事間隔很短、或隔天仍有高強度訓練，運動後越早補充足量碳水可能越有利。

如果恢復時間很長，例如距離下一次高強度訓練超過 24 小時，這篇研究本身不足以單獨證明「立即補充」仍然具有同等重要性。這需要後續文獻補強。

## Limitations and Cautions

- 樣本只有 12 名男性自行車手，不能直接外推到女性、阻力訓練、球類運動員、一般健身者或臨床族群。
- 研究觀察窗口只有運動後 4 小時，較適合討論早期恢復，不等於完整 24-48 小時恢復。
- 本頁目前依 PubMed 摘要與書目資訊整理，尚未讀取全文方法與統計細節。
- 這篇研究支持的是「肌糖原恢復」情境，不應直接延伸成所有營養目標都存在狹窄 anabolic window。
