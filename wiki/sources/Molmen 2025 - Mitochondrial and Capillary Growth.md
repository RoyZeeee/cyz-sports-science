# Molmen 2025 - Mitochondrial and Capillary Growth

## Source

- Title: Effects of Exercise Training on Mitochondrial and Capillary Growth in Human Skeletal Muscle: A Systematic Review and Meta-Regression
- Authors: Knut Sindre Mølmen, Niels W. Almquist, Øyvind Skattebo
- Journal: Sports Medicine, 2025;55(1):115-144
- Publication type: Systematic review and meta-regression
- PMID: 39390310
- PMCID: PMC11787188
- DOI: 10.1007/s40279-024-02120-2
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39390310/

## Summary

這篇系統綜述與 meta-regression 比較不同有氧訓練型態對人類骨骼肌粒線體含量與微血管化的影響。作者納入低到中等強度連續耐力訓練（ET）、高強度間歇或連續訓練（HIT）與衝刺間歇訓練（SIT），並同時整理 VO2max、肌纖維橫截面積與肌纖維類型比例。

研究重點是：粒線體與微血管是有氧適能的重要周邊基礎。不同強度模式都能促進粒線體含量提升；但微血管化的適應較不容易只用「訓練負荷 = 量 x 強度」解釋。

## Data Scope

- Search databases: PubMed, Web of Science, SPORTDiscus.
- Search date: up to 2022-02-02.
- Included interventions: ET, HIT, SIT.
- Mitochondrial quantitative synthesis: data from 5973 participants across 353 research articles.
- Capillary quantitative synthesis: data from 131 research articles.
- Secondary outcomes: VO2max, muscle fiber cross-sectional area, fiber type proportion.

## Key Findings

- After covariate adjustment, mitochondrial content increased similarly after ET, HIT, and SIT.
- Reported mitochondrial content changes:
  - ET: about 23 +/- 5%.
  - HIT: about 27 +/- 5%.
  - SIT: about 27 +/- 7%.
- These mitochondrial adaptations were not clearly influenced by age, sex, menopause, disease status, or the amount of muscle mass engaged.
- Training load, defined broadly as volume x intensity, was a useful predictor of mitochondrial content and VO2max changes.
- The relationship between training load and capillary adaptations was less clear.

## Practical Interpretation

這篇文獻削弱了「只有很高強度才會有效提升有氧能力」的簡化觀點。ET、HIT、SIT 都能提升粒線體含量，代表低到中等強度耐力訓練也有明確生理價值。

對 CrossFit、Hyrox、格鬥、球類與混合體能訓練者來說，這意味著訓練不應永遠停留在高強度輸出。高強度訓練可能快速推升 VO2max，但較低強度或可累積的耐力訓練可能更適合建立長期有氧基礎、恢復能力與周邊氧氣輸送環境。

## Limitations and Cautions

- 這是跨研究整合，原始研究的測量方法、族群與訓練處方差異很大。
- 結果比較的是平均適應，不代表每位運動員對 ET、HIT、SIT 的反應相同。
- 粒線體含量、微血管化與 VO2max 相關，但不等同於特定運動項目的表現。
- 微血管化與訓練負荷關係較不清楚，因此不宜只用「總負荷越高越好」推論。
