# Tan 2023 - Combining Aerobic and Heavy Resistance Training

## Source

- Title: The Effects of Combining Aerobic and Heavy Resistance Training on Body Composition, Muscle Hypertrophy, and Exercise Satisfaction in Physically Active Adults
- Authors: Jerrican Tan, Oleksandr Krasilshchikov, Garry Kuan, Hairul Anuar Hashim, Monira I. Aldhahi, Sameer Badri Al-Mhanna, Georgian Badicu
- Journal: Healthcare, 2023;11(17):2443
- DOI: 10.3390/healthcare11172443
- PMCID: PMC10487730
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10487730/

## Summary

這篇研究比較 8 週「重阻力肌肥大訓練」與「重阻力肌肥大訓練加有氧訓練」對身體組成、肌肥大相關圍度與運動滿意度的影響。受試者為 18-35 歲、健康、身體活動量較高但肌肥大導向訓練經驗有限的男性。

研究結果顯示，合併有氧與阻力訓練組在體重、體脂率、脂肪量與瘦體重等體組成變數上有較多組內改善；但兩組之間沒有顯著交互作用或明確組間差異。肌肥大相關圍度的結果較混合：胸圍兩組都改善，肩圍與臀圍主要在阻力組改善，大腿圍主要在合併組改善。兩組運動滿意度都提高，但組間沒有顯著差異。

## Study Design

- Design: 8-week intervention study.
- Participants: 22 healthy physically active male adults analyzed, 11 per group after dropouts.
- Age range: 18-35 years.
- Setting: workout studio in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.
- Groups:
  - Resistance training only.
  - Resistance training plus aerobic training.
- Resistance protocol: 3 sessions/week, hypertrophy-oriented, 10-12 RM, 60 s rest, multi-exercise split.
- Aerobic addition: 3 sessions/week, 30-35 min interval aerobic training, with 2 min jogging at 60-70% HRR and 4 min recovery at 50-60% HRR for 4 rounds.
- Outcomes:
  - Body composition via bioelectrical impedance.
  - Limb and trunk girths as hypertrophy proxies.
  - Exercise satisfaction via PACES.

## Key Findings

- Combined training showed significant within-group reductions in body fat percentage and fat mass.
- Combined training showed significant within-group improvements in lean body mass.
- No significant group-by-time interactions were observed for body composition variables.
- Chest girth improved in both groups.
- Shoulder and hip girth improved significantly only in the resistance training group.
- Thigh girth improved significantly only in the combined training group.
- No significant group-by-time interactions were observed for muscle hypertrophy variables.
- Exercise satisfaction improved within both groups, without significant between-group difference.

## Practical Interpretation

這篇研究不支持簡單說法「加有氧一定會殺死肌肥大」。在這個 8 週、低到中等有氧量、以健康活躍男性為對象的設計中，加入有氧訓練沒有顯示出明確的組間干擾效應，並可能對體脂下降、瘦體重與大腿圍有利。

更保守的說法是：如果阻力訓練仍是主體、有氧量與強度可控，併行訓練可以是改善體組成且不明顯犧牲訓練滿意度的策略。

## Limitations and Cautions

- 樣本很小，最終分析每組 11 人。
- 僅納入 18-35 歲健康男性，不能直接外推到女性、老年人、肥胖族群、精英運動員或復健族群。
- 肌肥大使用圍度作為代理指標，缺少超音波、MRI、DXA 或肌肉厚度等更直接測量。
- 兩組訓練總時間不同，合併組多了有氧訓練時間，因此很難區分「訓練模式」與「總運動量」的效果。
- 介入僅 8 週，不足以判斷長期肌肥大或力量發展。
- 結果多為組內改善，組間差異不明顯；實務解讀應避免過度宣稱合併訓練優於阻力訓練。

## Open Questions

- 若有氧訓練量更高、強度更高或接近阻力訓練時間，干擾效應是否會更明顯？
- 對已有多年肌肥大訓練經驗者，合併訓練是否仍能維持同樣結果？
- 有氧與阻力訓練的排序、間隔時間與運動模式是否改變適應？
- 用 DXA、超音波或 MRI 測量肌肉量時，結果是否仍一致？
